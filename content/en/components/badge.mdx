A short, non-interactive label for a status or a category.

It renders a plain `<span>` with no ARIA role, because that is what it is: text. Nothing announces
it as a widget, nothing puts it in the tab order, and nothing implies it can be pressed.

Everything a `<span>` accepts is accepted too, `ref` included.

## Colour is never the message

`tone` is the one prop worth being careful with. A red badge reading "Failed" survives being
printed in black and white, being read aloud, and being looked at by the eight percent of men who
will not see it as red. A red badge reading "Build" does not.

The rule this component asks for is short: write the word first, then choose the tone that agrees
with it. If the tone is doing work the word is not, the word is wrong.

## Not a button, not a filter

A badge that can be clicked is a button wearing a badge's clothes, and readers find out the hard
way — by tabbing past it, or by clicking and getting nothing. If it removes a filter, it is a
`Button` with a badge's styling. If it selects one, it is a control with a pressed state. Both are
worth building on purpose; neither is this.
