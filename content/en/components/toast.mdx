A short message about something that has already happened, raised from wherever it happened.

This is the one place the library holds state. A message raised from anywhere has to be rendered
somewhere above everything, so `ToastProvider` owns the queue it renders. That is the exception,
and it is scoped to the toasts themselves.

## `useToast`

Returns `{ toast, dismiss }`. `toast(options)` raises one and returns its id; `dismiss(id)` takes
it back before its time is up.

## What a toast is not

It is not a way to ask a question, and it is not a way to report an error someone has to act on. A
toast leaves on its own, and anything that leaves on its own cannot be the only place something
important was said. If the reader has to do something about it, it belongs on the page — a
`Dialog`, or an `Alert` next to what it concerns.
