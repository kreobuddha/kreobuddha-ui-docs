A bubble of extra information, opened by clicking a button.

It exists because a tooltip cannot carry anything that matters.

## Why this exists

A tooltip opens on hover, and there is no hover on a touchscreen. The library accepted that as a
property of the pattern rather than a defect — which leaves everything a reader actually needs
without a home. This is that home.

Same top layer, same CSS anchor positioning. The difference is that it is opened on purpose, which
is what makes it safe to put something load-bearing inside.

## The trigger has to be a button

Not a span with a click handler, not an icon. `aria-expanded` is added to whatever is passed, and
that attribute is a lie on anything that is not announced as a control and reachable by keyboard.

Focus returns to the trigger when the bubble closes, so a keyboard reader ends up where they
started rather than at the top of the document.

## The content is mounted only while open

It sits in a `role="status"` region, and the region is empty until the button is pressed.

That ordering is the whole trick. A live region announces what *changes* inside it, so content that
was always there and merely revealed is announced by nothing at all — the reader presses the button
and hears silence. Mounting on open makes the appearance a change, and the change is what gets
read.
