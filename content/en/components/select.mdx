## The trade this component makes

The native element was chosen over a custom listbox deliberately, and it is worth knowing what that
buys and what it costs.

It brings the platform's keyboard behaviour, its type-ahead, form participation, and — on a phone —
the operating system's own picker, which no custom widget matches. What it gives up is control over
how the open list looks. Only the closed control is styled here. The chevron is drawn by the
component, because the platform paints a different arrow on every OS.

Use it when one answer comes from a list too long to show as radios. For a handful of options
someone should be able to compare at a glance, `Radio` inside a `FieldGroup` is the better shape.

## `placeholder` is not a value

It becomes a disabled first `<option>` rather than a nullable value: visible until a choice is
made, unselectable afterwards. That is what lets the native `required` attribute mean what it says,
instead of a form accepting "Choose one" as an answer.
