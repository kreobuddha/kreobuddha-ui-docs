Text that runs to several lines — a description, a note, a pasted log.

It is `TextField`'s counterpart and shares its whole field contract.

Everything a `<textarea>` accepts is accepted too, except `prefix`.

## Height is `rows`, not `size`

`size` sets the padding, and the type is `--kreo-type-body` at every size — so height had to come
from somewhere, and `rows` is where the platform already puts it. A field can then be as tall as the
answer deserves rather than as tall as its padding scale allows.

## Horizontal dragging is never offered

`resize` chooses between vertical and none. There is no horizontal option, and that is a decision
rather than an omission: dragging a field wider breaks the form's grid, and a wider box is not
easier to read — line length has an upper bound past which the eye loses the start of the next line.

## `Textarea` or `TextField`

Reach for this when the answer has no reason to fit on one line, and for `TextField` when it does.

<Callout tone="warning">
  A one-line answer in a three-line box invites a paragraph nobody wanted, and then somebody has to
  decide what to do with it.
</Callout>

## The field contract is shared

Same required `label`, same `hint`, same rule that `error` *is* the invalid state, same body type.
That is deliberate: a form built from both should not read as a form built from two libraries.
