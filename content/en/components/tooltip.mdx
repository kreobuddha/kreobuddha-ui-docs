## There is no hover on a phone

A tooltip opens on hover and on focus. A touchscreen has neither in the way this pattern needs, so
on a phone this component effectively does not exist.

The library treats that as a property of the pattern rather than a defect to work around, and the
rule that follows is short: **anything a reader actually needs belongs in a label, a hint, or
visible text.** A tooltip is for the icon button whose meaning is already obvious to nine readers
in ten.

When the content matters, the component is `Toggletip` — a button the reader presses, which works
by keyboard and by touch.

## The delay is asymmetric

Opening is delayed; closing is not.

A pointer crossing a row of icon buttons would otherwise set off a chain of tooltips behind it, and
one that lingers after the pointer has moved on is in the way of whatever it covers. Waiting to
open costs a reader who paused nothing; waiting to close costs everyone else.

Positioning uses CSS anchor positioning, so the browser flips the placement when there is no room
rather than the library measuring the viewport and guessing.
