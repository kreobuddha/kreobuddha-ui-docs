## Spacing

A 4px base. The number in the token name is the step, not the pixel value: `--kreo-space-4` is
16px.

**Page layout tokens are deliberately not published.** Gutters, page widths and rail widths belong
to the application, which knows its own layout; a component library guessing them would be wrong
everywhere at once.

## Borders and focus

Separation is carried by borders, not by elevation: there is no shadow on cards or controls.
Focus is a 2px accent border drawn **inside** the box, so gaining focus never reflows the layout.

<TokenTable include={['--kreo-border-w', '--kreo-focus-ring']} />

## Elevation

One shadow, for things that float — menus, dialogs, tooltips. Nothing anchored to the page carries
it. Overlays keep a border as well: forced-colors mode paints no shadow at all, and a panel
relying on one would lose its edge exactly where an edge matters most. The dark theme uses a
deeper shadow, because a charcoal surface swallows a light one.
